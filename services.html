Container Basics

FCL, LCL or Air Consolidation

FCL — Full Container

Your cargo fills a whole container. Best for volume and a lower per-unit cost.

LCL — Shared Container

Your goods share a container with others. Ideal for smaller consignments.

Air Consolidation

Smaller air shipments combined to bring down the per-kg rate.

Customs Clearance

Two Directions, Two Checklists

Importing

What happens when cargo arrives.

  • Documents checked against the cargo
  • Goods classified and duty assessed
  • Bill of Entry filed
  • Duty paid and cargo released
  • Transport to your door

Exporting

What happens before cargo leaves.

  • Goods packed and marked
  • Shipping Bill filed
  • Customs inspection if selected
  • Cargo loaded and sealed
  • Transit documents released
